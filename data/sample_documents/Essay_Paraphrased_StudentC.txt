In distributed computation frameworks, consensus protocols permit an interconnected cluster of computing entities to establish collective agreement on mutable state values even during catastrophic communication disruptions. 
The Raft architecture renders consensus comprehensible by fragmenting the algorithmic process into distinct sub-problems: designated authority selection, sequential journal propagation, and operational invariants.
A presiding coordinator is designated through stochastic interval timers so as to diminish simultaneous ballot deadlocks.
Following confirmation of dominance, all transactional operations are channeled exclusively through the designated authority prior to persistent recording in subordinate registers.
